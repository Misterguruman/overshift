THE LEDGER

Every cleared contract can run in a bay and earn credits, even while the game is closed (up to one shift, 8 hours to start). A solution at par earns the contract's base rate; fewer cycles and fewer lines earn more. The Ledger is the first page of the contract board: L1 / R1 flip pages (Q / E), or tap ₵. L2 / R2 or ← → switch between Bays and Upgrades, ↑ ↓ pick, A moves a contract in or out of a bay, Start opens it to improve it, hold A to buy an upgrade, B goes back to the board. Upgrades grow the business and never change a contract.

CHAPTER 2 · JUNCTION FLOOR

Build mode: the d-pad moves the tile cursor, hold X + ← → picks a part, tap X turns it, A places (hold A and move to draw a belt), B removes, Y undoes. Tap tiles on either screen to place. Program mode: the editor works as in chapter 1 on one node; L1 + ← → or a tab switches nodes. A belt takes cells from its back and sides; a node only from its back. A node shows an arrow on each side its program sends cells out of, and a ? until it has a program: put the cursor on it and press L3 (or tap the tip) to program it.
